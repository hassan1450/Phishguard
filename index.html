    How it works

    The 7-stage supervised learning workflow from the research proposal.

    1

    Dataset collection

    Labeled phishing and legitimate URLs from a public dataset.

    2

    Preprocessing

    Remove duplicates and missing values; convert labels to 0/1.

    3

    Feature selection

    URL length, subdomains, IP usage, HTTPS, suspicious words, entropy and more.

    4

    Data splitting

    Stratified 80:20 split plus an unseen-domain split to test generalization.

    5

    Model training

    Logistic Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest on identical data.

    6

    Evaluation

    Accuracy, precision, recall, F1 and confusion matrices (phishing = positive class).

    7

    Prediction

    The scanner extracts the same features from a new URL and classifies it.

    PhishGuard · BSIT research project · predictions are probabilistic, not guarantees
